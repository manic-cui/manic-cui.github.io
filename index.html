Manni Cui

Undergraduate at HUST, exchange student at UC Berkeley

I am a third-year undergraduate at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), studying Electronic Information and Communication Engineering in the Innovation Experimental Class (Advanced Mathematics and Physics Track). For the 2026–27 academic year I am an exchange student at UC Berkeley.

I currently work on robotics and embodied AI at UC Berkeley. Before that, I worked on AI-generated content (AIGC) detection as a research intern in the Security Department of Ant Group and as a remote research assistant at the Pattern Recognition Laboratory, Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences.
